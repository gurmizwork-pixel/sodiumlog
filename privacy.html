Privacy Policy

SodiumLog – Daily Salt Tracker · Last updated: May 2026

Solstice Digital Apps ("we", "us") built SodiumLog as a free app. This page explains our privacy practices.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. SodiumLog stores all information exclusively on your device using your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • Your sodium log entries (food names, amounts, timestamps)
  • Your daily goal setting
  • Your saved recent and favourite items

None of this data is transmitted to any server, shared with third parties, or accessible by us.

No Account Required

SodiumLog does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

Advertising

The free version of SodiumLog displays a banner advertisement placeholder. In a future update, real ads may be served by Google AdMob. If AdMob is integrated, Google's privacy policy will apply to ad delivery. You can remove all ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

In-App Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information. Google Play's terms and privacy policy govern payment processing.

Children

SodiumLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
